Performance arc — eight registers

01ConfidenceOwns the room without working for it.
02CuriosityInterest that hasn't yet admitted it is fear.

04TrustThe rarest note. Costs her something to play.

Top Right · Relationship Journey

Five stages with the male lead

The bar is proximity — how much distance she allows. It closes over four stages and reopens in one cut.

SuspicionDistance 100%
Two-shots with air between them. She keeps exits in frame.
UtilityDistance 70%
Shared table, shared evidence. Hands near, never touching.
TrustDistance 40%
Singles start matching. She looks at him when he isn't looking.
ProximityDistance 10%
Foreheads close, hand to face, a held embrace. Near-kiss framing — the kiss never lands.
RuptureDistance 100%
Back to the widest lens in the film. The room does the acting.
REF · 002 PROXIMITY Two-shot — the last inch 50mm · T1.4 · practical lamp key · both eyelines held
Standing direction

Tension, not contact

  • No kiss anywhere in the piece.
  • No bed scenes, in any look, including nightwear.
  • Intimacy is built from eyeline, proximity, breath, hands and the cut — never from removal of clothing.
  • Every intimate setup is blocked and agreed with both performers before the camera rolls.

Dedicated Section · Resort / Swimwear Visual Reference

Eight setups at the pool — glamour with a watcher in it

Not a swimwear shoot with a story attached. Ayla is at the resort chasing a clue; the sequence is written so the audience realises, halfway through, that the camera is standing where someone else is standing.

Wardrobe & consent rider — applies to every setup below

Approved, covered, closed

  • Swimwear style, cut and coverage agreed and signed off in pre-production. Nothing is introduced on the day.
  • Cover-up or robe on set for every setup and worn between takes.
  • Closed set: director, DOP, 1st AC, stylist, and the performer's own representative. No stills photographer without prior notice.
  • Framing is full-body, editorial and architectural. No isolated body-part coverage, no low-angle or over-the-shoulder body inserts.
  • Performer holds the right to review and withdraw any frame from the delivered selects.
  • Rated 18+ for theme and tension. Tasteful and glamorous — never explicit.

Dedicated Section · Romantic Visual Reference

Intimacy built from distance, not contact

Everything in this sequence is achieved with lens, eyeline and cut. The performers stay clothed, the bed stays out of frame, and the kiss never happens — the audience should feel it about to, twice, and be denied both times.

RM · 01SEATEDSitting close, face to face40mm · T2 · single lamp key · warm falloff

Knees angled in, shoulders square. The blocking says trust before either line is spoken.

RM · 02EYESClose-up — held eyeline100mm · T1.8 · catchlight from window · no cutaway

Run it long. The performance is in what changes across eight seconds of not looking away.

RM · 03NEAR-KISSForeheads almost touching50mm · T1.4 · profile two-shot · rim from city light

Blocked to an agreed gap and held there. Cut out on the breath, never on contact.

RM · 04HANDSHand at the side of the face85mm · T2 · insert · shallow · practical only

Hands do the work the mouths are not allowed to. Agreed choreography, rehearsed cold.

RM · 05EMBRACEGentle embrace, shoulder framing65mm · T2 · shoulder/neck level · back three-quarter

Framed at the shoulder. Her face is what the audience is watching, not the hold.

RM · 06SILENCELooking at each other, saying nothing50mm · T2.8 · symmetrical two-shot · lamp + city

The scene's true climax. No dialogue, no music cue, no movement.

RM · 07AFTERWalking together, afterward32mm · T4 · wide · night exterior · practical wash

Wide and quiet. The last frame before the discrepancy lands — plant the ease so the rupture costs something.

RM · 08MIRRORAlone at the mirror75mm · T2 · reflection single · nightwear + robe

Private interior, reflection scene. She is assessing herself the way she assesses evidence.
